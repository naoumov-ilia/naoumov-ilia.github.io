## Evident Assertion

Only those f2i can be the termination of a secondary wave movement from which the line parallel to the supply line (defined by the values v1 and v2(t)) does not intersect the chart before the correction channel ends.

## Extension

An extension occurs when the supply line is intersected by the chart in one direction and becomes a support line.

There are two types of extension:

- type 1 — defined in Article 1; the channel extension is determined by the admissible slope of the demand line;
- type 2 — the supply line (connecting v1 and v2(t)) is intersected in one direction, becoming a support line
